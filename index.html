About

I am an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Grand Valley State University. My teaching and research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning, operating systems, and making complex CS topics accessible and fun for my students.

Teaching

Courses that I regularly teach/involved in:

  • AI 201 Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
  • AI 421/521 Applied Computer Vision
  • CIS 263 Data Structures and Algorithms
  • CIS 352 Operating System Essentials
  • CIS 365 Applied Artificial Intelligence (retired)
  • CIS 378 Applied Machine Learning
  • CIS 452 Operating Systems Concepts

Research

My research areas of interest are pattern recognition, applied machine learning, artificial intelligence, biometrics, generative art and computer vision.
